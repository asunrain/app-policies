Introduction

PixKit (package name com.sun.rain.pixkit) is an Android image toolbox. It gathers a set of everyday image utilities into one app: compressing photos, converting between image formats, turning photos into a PDF, building collages, adding text watermarks, resizing, cropping and rotating, and reading image information such as dimensions and EXIF data.

Every one of these tools runs entirely on your device. PixKit has no user account, no server of its own and no cloud service: your images are never uploaded anywhere. The app also works with the network switched off — only the optional reward video described in section 3 needs a connection.

This privacy policy explains what information is processed when you use PixKit, why it is processed and what choices you have. By installing and using the app you acknowledge the practices described below.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, upload or store any personal data on our own servers. Specifically:

  • No account: there is no registration and no login. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or password.
  • No analytics of our own: we do not embed our own analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and we do not build a profile of you.
  • Your images stay on your device: the photos you select are decoded and processed in your device's memory. They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and we keep no copy.
  • Your settings stay on your device: your preferences (appearance, language, saving behaviour, how many free runs you have used today, and whether the app is unlocked) are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Exported files stay on your device: the results you save are written into your own storage, as described in section 6.

There is one exception: advertising, where the Google Mobile Ads SDK processes certain data automatically. This is described in section 3.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

PixKit is free of charge and is supported by advertising delivered by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK).

We use a single ad format: an optional rewarded video. It is never shown automatically and never interrupts your work. After you have used the free runs included in the app for the day, you may choose to watch a short video to unlock additional runs. Watching is always your choice, and if no video is available the app keeps working.

To deliver and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share the following information:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) and app set ID;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate, city-level location may be derived;
  • device type, operating system version, app version, language and other technical information;
  • information about the ad shown to you and your interaction with it.

This information is used for advertising and marketing, for measuring ad performance and for fraud prevention, and it may be shared with Google's advertising partners. It is transmitted over encrypted connections.

To be clear: the ad SDK never receives your photos, your exported files or the contents of your gallery.

You can control advertising identifiers and personalised advertising on your device at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads) — delete or reset the advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation.
  • Where the law requires it (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland), personalised advertising relies on your consent, which you give or refuse through the consent form presented in the app before advertising identifiers are used. You can change your choice at any time.

For more information about how Google processes this data:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Apart from Google AdMob, PixKit does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking service.

How We Use Information

We do not use your personal data, because we do not collect any. The limited information processed by the advertising SDK is used solely to display the reward video, to measure its performance and to prevent fraud, as described in section 3.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for any purpose unrelated to running the app and its advertising.

App Permissions

PixKit requests as few permissions as possible. The complete list declared by the app is:

  • android.permission.INTERNET — used only to load the optional reward video. Every tool in the app works without it.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — used by the ad SDK to check whether a connection is available before requesting an ad.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — allows the ad SDK to access the advertising identifier, as required from Android 13 onwards.
  • ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS — declared by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for the Privacy Sandbox on Android. They concern advertising, not your photos.
  • PixKit also inherits one internal AndroidX permission (DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION), which only protects the app's own internal broadcasts and gives no access to user data.

PixKit does not request: camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, precise or approximate location, phone, SMS, body sensors, or any photo, media or storage permission. In particular the app does not declare READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WAKE_LOCK or FOREGROUND_SERVICE.

Your Photos and Files

Because PixKit is an image tool, this section deserves its own explanation.

  • How images reach the app: only through the Android system photo picker — the system's own photo dialogue. PixKit never gains access to your photo library as a whole; it can only see the individual images you explicitly select.
  • Where they are processed: in your device's memory, on your device. There is no upload step, no queue and no background transfer.
  • Where results are saved: into your own storage — images in Pictures/PixKit and PDFs in Downloads/PixKit, or into a folder you choose yourself through the system file dialogue. You can delete them at any time with any file manager or gallery app.
  • Originals are left untouched: every tool writes a new file. PixKit never overwrites or modifies the images you selected.
  • No cloud, no account: PixKit has no server component. Nothing you process is sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • Offline by design: put your device in aeroplane mode and every tool still works. Only the optional reward video described in section 3 needs a connection.
  • Your responsibility: please only process images you have the right to use. PixKit is a general-purpose tool and grants no rights to third-party material.

The image-information tool only reads what is already stored inside the file — for example the camera model, exposure settings or a location tag — and shows it to you locally. It never transmits this information and never modifies the file.

Data Storage and Security

All app data is stored locally on your device, in the app's private storage. No copy exists on any server.

The locally stored data consists of: your appearance and language preferences, your preferred saving behaviour, the number of free runs you have used today, and whether the app is unlocked.

Exported images and PDFs are written to the locations described in section 6 and remain there until you delete them.

Uninstalling the app removes its settings. You can also erase them at any time through Android Settings → Apps → PixKit → Storage → Clear storage. Exported files are not removed by uninstalling the app; delete them from your gallery or file manager.

No storage permission is required for any of this. We apply reasonable technical measures to protect the data we control; please note that no method of storage is completely secure.

Your rights: you have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict the processing of your personal data; the right to data portability; the right to object to processing; and the right to withdraw consent at any time. How to exercise them: contact us at android.sun.rain@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

Data controller: the developer of PixKit, reachable at the address above. We have not appointed a data protection officer, as we do not carry out processing that would require one.

Because PixKit itself does not collect personal data, requests concerning advertising data are best directed to Google as the provider of the ad SDK. We will assist you wherever we can.

Your California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, the CCPA/CPRA gives you specific rights regarding your personal information.

  • In the past 12 months the advertising SDK may have 'shared' identifiers such as the advertising ID with advertising partners for cross-context behavioural advertising, as described in section 3. We do not sell personal information for monetary value.
  • You have the right to opt out of the sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising. The simplest ways are to disable ads personalisation or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (section 3), or to refuse consent in the consent form when it is shown.
  • You also have the right to know, to delete and to correct your personal information, and to be free from discrimination for exercising these rights.

To make a request, contact us at android.sun.rain@gmail.com. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16 years of age.

Children's Privacy

PixKit is a general-purpose utility app and is not directed to children under 13, or under 16 where local law requires it. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app contains no user accounts, no chat, no messaging, no social features and no user-generated content.

Parents and guardians can additionally disable ads personalisation on the device, as described in section 3.

If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Data Retention

We keep no personal data on our servers. Data stored locally on your device remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Files you exported remain in your own storage until you delete them. Data processed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is retained in accordance with Google's own retention policies.

International Data Transfers

Advertising data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your country of residence, including the United States, by Google and its partners. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, including its UK extension.

PixKit itself transfers nothing, because it transmits no personal data at all.
